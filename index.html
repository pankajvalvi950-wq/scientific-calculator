SciCalc 991

Online scientific calculator with natural fractions, trig, logs, permutations and memory.

pankajvalvi@proton.me
991 PLUS · 2nd edition

Press SHIFT to use the yellow functions. DRG switches degrees, radians and grads. SHIFT + DRG or SHIFT + S⇔D changes the number of decimal places (FIX 0 to 10, then back to normal). S⇔D shows a decimal answer as a fraction. BASE switches the keys to number-system mode: DEC, HEX, BIN, OCT, A–F and logic operations appear on the keys, and the other bases are shown under the result. Press BASE again to go back. Your keyboard works too: digits, + − * / ^ ( ), Enter, Backspace, Esc, arrows.
